YARI CHEQUE MANAGER

Privacy Policy

✓ No data collection · No internet required

YARI CHEQUE MANAGER is a fully offline desktop application. All your data stays on your device — we never collect, transmit, or store any information on external servers.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. YARI CHEQUE MANAGER does not:

  • Require an account or registration
  • Connect to the internet
  • Send any data to our servers or third parties
  • Use analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry
  • Use cookies or tracking technologies

2. Data Storage

All data entered in the app (bank details, cheque books, cheque records, print templates) is stored exclusively on your local device in a SQLite database file located at:

%APPDATA%\YARI CHEQUE MANAGER\yari_cheque_mgr.db

This file remains on your device under your full control. Uninstalling the application does not automatically delete this file, allowing you to reinstall without losing your data.

3. Third-Party Services

YARI CHEQUE MANAGER does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or cloud platforms. No data leaves your device.

4. Children's Privacy

This application does not collect data from any users, including children under the age of 13.
